Pain Points
1
Assignment employees (~50) Critical
All automated integrations fail for assignees - fully manual processing required. Each assignee involves a home country and a host country; Client runs shadow payroll on the home side for all assignments across all countries. The HR / ERP System → Payroll Aggregator integration (via PG XML / pay group routing) can only route an employee to one country at a time, so assignees cannot be active in Payroll Aggregator for both jurisdictions simultaneously. Shadow payroll employees are created as duplicate records in Payroll Aggregator (e.g. Sweden creates a new employee that exists only in Payroll Aggregator for shadow calculation purposes; other countries add shadow elements to the existing record). Additional data - term sheets and mobility inputs from Mobility Advisor (mobility/tax advisor) - lives in Google Drive and Dropbox, not in HR / ERP System. ~2.5% of headcount drives disproportionate manual effort each cycle.
2
Reconciliation of discrepancies
Payroll Aggregator provides an instant reconciliation view comparing inputs submitted versus results returned from External Partner. When provider outputs differ from inputs, each discrepancy must be investigated manually - identifying which person an extra amount belongs to, understanding why the amount changed, and deciding whether to accept or reject it. For assignees, it is especially hard to determine what the correct figure should be, given the complexity of cross-jurisdiction adjustments and host-country allowance changes feeding back into home-country shadow payroll.
3
Payroll helpdesk - manual triage and resolution
The Central Operations payroll team uses Zendesk to manage all employee payroll queries ("this is wrong on my Payroll Aggregator", "when do I get paid", "why has my tax changed", etc.). Every step is manual: a person opens each ticket, reads and classifies the query, assigns a category, routes it to the correct country-responsible specialist, and the specialist then investigates manually - looking up HR / ERP System or Payroll Aggregator records - before responding. There is no auto-triage, no automated response for routine queries, and no pattern detection across the ticket backlog. Some months of query history now exist, making this a viable automation candidate.
4
Knowledge concentration risk
Process knowledge is largely undocumented and held by a small number of individuals. In-country expertise leaving in waves: UK already gone; Stefan Hjerne (Head of Payroll, 20 years' experience) departing June; Germany & Luxembourg also departing June; APAC extended to September. Stefan's June deadline creates a firm and urgent timeline for knowledge capture and process codification.
