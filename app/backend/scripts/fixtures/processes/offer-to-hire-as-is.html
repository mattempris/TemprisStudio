Process Map Talent Acquisition

Offer to Hire Work Flow

  • After the candidate signs his/her offer letter, the recruiting coordinator gets a task to progress the candidate to background screening
  • Once this task is submitted, the process is passed over to the background screening team
  • The background screening team may ask the HR coordinator to request additional information from the candidate
  • When the background check is complete, the process back to TA Ops to complete the hire
