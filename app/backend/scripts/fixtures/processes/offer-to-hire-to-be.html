Offer to Hire - Reimagined

  • On offer signature, the Agent automatically detects the event and initiates background screening - no coordinator action required
  • Background check exceptions are handled by the Agent; the HR Coordinator provides a single-click approval of an AI-drafted candidate communication in ChatGPT Enterprise
  • The screening team completes a Four Eyed Check supported by an AI-prepared adjudication brief, reducing review time from days to under an hour
  • On clearance, the Agent pre-populates the Workday hire action and presents it to TA Ops for single-click approval; all onboarding workflows trigger automatically
